Daylink · Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-20 · Richard Lee

Records stored on your device

Daylink 1.0.0 stores your tasks, habits, goals, schedules, repetition rules, completion and undo events, date changes, language and color preferences in local app storage. The app works without an account and does not send these records to an operator server.

File backups and restore

Choosing file backup exports your records as a JSON file to a location you select in the iOS Files interface. Choosing iCloud Drive or another file provider is subject to that service’s settings and policies. The app does not encrypt exported JSON files. Keep them in a trusted location. Restoring replaces current app records; a local recovery copy of the state before the last restore is kept. iOS device backups are managed separately by Apple and your device settings.

Advertising and analytics

This version has no advertisements, advertising tracking SDK, operator-run usage analytics or third-party sign-in. If you use TestFlight or Apple diagnostics and feedback, information you share may be provided through Apple under its policies.

Support email and feedback

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the contents and attachments you choose to send, to respond and investigate the issue. You do not need to send personal tasks or goals. Email is handled through the operator’s Naver mailbox. Support correspondence held by the operator is deleted within one year after the inquiry is resolved.

Keeping and deleting records

Deleting an item can retain its past execution history so that your records remain consistent. Deleting the app through iOS removes its local data and recovery copy; offloading the app can retain data. Exported files and device or cloud backups must be managed separately in the selected storage service. There is no Daylink account or server copy to delete. You can request access, correction or deletion of correspondence held by the operator using the email below.

This support website

These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, forms or custom tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes under GitHub’s policies. The app does not send your planner records to this website.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date if features or data practices change. Operator: Richard Lee. For privacy questions or requests, email fortuneagain@naver.com.
